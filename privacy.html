Privacy Policy

Operation Costa — Google Drive Connector

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Overview

Operation Costa is a privately operated, local research and document management system. Its Google Drive connector is used for authorized document downloads and encrypted backups.

Google Drive Permissions

The research connection requests read-only access to Google Drive files. The backup connection requests permission to create and manage files associated with the application.

Access is granted by the account owner through Google's OAuth authorization process.

How Data Is Used

Authorized documents may be downloaded to privately operated hardware for local research and processing. Backup data is encrypted locally before being uploaded to Google Drive.

Google Drive is not used to perform AI inference.

Data Storage and Security

Local research data and processing outputs are managed on privately operated systems. The backup connector uses client-side encryption to protect backup contents and filenames before upload.

OAuth credentials and encryption credentials are managed separately from this public website.

Data Sharing

The connector is intended for private use. It does not sell Google user data or use Google Drive information for advertising.

Google Drive remains the external storage provider for authorized transfers and encrypted backups.

Retention and Deletion

Downloaded documents and backups are retained according to the system owner's storage and backup management decisions. Files may be removed from local storage or Google Drive by the account owner.

Revoking Access

The account owner can revoke the connector's Google account access through their Google Account security settings. Existing locally downloaded files and previously uploaded backups are managed separately.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated if the connector's functionality, permissions, or data handling changes.
